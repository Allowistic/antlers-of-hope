NEIGHBORS HELPING NEIGHBORS

A place with purpose.

In early 2024, Mike and Rhonda Harvey took a chance on the old Antlers property sitting above Success Lake on Highway 190. What started as an idea for an event space became something deeper — a permanent home for their nonprofit, Helping One Person Everywhere Inc.

Antlers of Hope opened its doors March 15, 2024 as a down-to-earth bar and grill with a clear mission: great food, cold drinks, live music, and real community — all in service of helping families facing serious medical challenges.

Rhonda Harvey — President & Founder
“This place belongs to the community. We’re just stewards.”
THE VISION

From the grill to the cause.

A portion of every dollar spent at Antlers of Hope supports HOPE Inc. — funding direct aid, benefit dinners, and the future Big Red Barn event center and small RV park that will serve even more families.

THE JOURNEY HERE

The Mighty 190

You’ll find us on one of California’s most beautiful and beloved back roads — State Route 190, affectionately known by locals as the Mighty 190.

Just minutes east of Porterville and a short, scenic drive from the tiny, charming foothill town of Springville — the “Hidden Gateway” to Sequoia National Forest.

Whether you’re headed up to the Trail of 100 Giants, coming down from a day in the high country, or just cruising the river canyon, we’re the perfect stop for a cold drink, a hot meal, and some real conversation.

Antlers of Hope exists to support Helping One Person Everywhere Inc., the nonprofit founded by Rhonda Harvey to walk alongside families navigating serious illness, treatment, and loss.

When you eat here, drink here, sing here, or rent the space for an event — you are directly helping your neighbors.

Future: The Big Red Barn event venue & small RV park — more space to serve more families.
